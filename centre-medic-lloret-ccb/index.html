    About the centre

    A neighbourhood surgery, open all night.

    Centre Mèdic Lloret CCB sits at number 49 of Avenida Vila de Tossa, in Lloret de Mar. It is a Doctor 24 horas: primary care when you need it, whether you live on the Costa Brava or you are only here for the week.

    Dr. Pablo Padilla and the team treat everything from blocked ears to a bad day on the beach. Visitors say they were seen quickly, in English, and told the price and the wait up front.

    Consulting room shown in the photo.

    Before you come

    Questions, answered honestly.

    The centre offers walk-in and urgent primary care. If you prefer, you can request an appointment on this page or call 972 36 16 46.
    The signs on the building read "Medical Centre 24h" and "Doctor 24 horas". If you want to be certain before you set out, call 972 36 16 46.
    Patients report that the team speaks good English. For another language, ask when you call or write.
    We do not list prices online because they depend on the consultation. Patients say the staff is honest about cost, so ask at reception before you go ahead.
    One visitor reported that the wait and the procedure together took under 40 minutes. It varies with the day and the case.
